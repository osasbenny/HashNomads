# HashNomads — Phase 2 Readiness Gate

Phase 2 is **reputable hosting/data-center operator diligence and negotiation**. This document defines when we are ready to enter it; the detailed negotiation/profit model will be developed in the next workstream.

## Required before Phase 2
- V1 sandbox golden path operational.
- V1 evidence/test report complete.
- Customer and admin flows demonstrable.
- ASIC/facility/pool integration contracts documented.
- Proposed commercial/profit model approved internally.
- Initial target jurisdictions identified for legal review.
- No critical/high security blocker.
- Clear list of integration data/API requirements for facility operator.

## Data-center diligence categories
When Phase 2 begins, evaluate:
- corporate identity, track record and references;
- facility location/jurisdiction and site security;
- power source, tariff and pass-through methodology;
- minimum commitment/capacity reservation;
- supported ASIC models and electrical standards;
- cooling/environmental controls;
- network redundancy;
- uptime/curtailment SLA and credits;
- remote hands, repairs, firmware policy and spare parts;
- hardware receipt, serial verification, custody and chain of evidence;
- insurance/risk of loss;
- deployment lead times;
- pool freedom and API/telemetry access;
- billing/metering/reconciliation;
- shipping/import/export;
- termination, retrieval and relocation rights;
- audit/inspection rights;
- incident notification obligations;
- data/privacy/security requirements.

## Negotiation objective
Prefer an asset-light launch: customer-funded hardware procurement with transparent fees and an operator partnership, rather than HashNomads pre-purchasing a large fleet before demand is proven.

## Hard production gate
No facility may be shown as live/available for paid deployment until commercial agreement, verified capacity, operational integration, legal/compliance review and production acceptance tests are complete.
